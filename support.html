Does swiping left delete the photo?

No. Swiping left puts it in Sift's bin. Photos are only deleted when you open the bin, tap Delete and confirm in the iOS prompt.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes, for 30 days. Sift deletes through iOS, so deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them.

Will this delete photos from iCloud too?

If you use iCloud Photos, yes: deleting a photo removes it from every device signed in to your iCloud account, exactly like deleting it in the Photos app.

Why does it say "in iCloud" instead of a size?

With "Optimize iPhone Storage" on, some originals live only in iCloud. Deleting them frees iCloud space but no space on your iPhone, so Sift counts them separately.

Sift only shows some of my photos

You gave Sift access to selected photos. Tap "Choose more" in Sift, or go to Settings › Apps › Sift › Photos and choose Full Access.

Are favorites skipped?

Yes, by default. You can include them in Sift's settings.

Does Sift upload my photos?

No. Sift has no servers and never uploads or shares a photo. If a photo is stored only in iCloud, iOS downloads it from your own iCloud so you can see it sharply.
