Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Sift does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

Your photos

Sift reads your photo library on your device only to show you your photos and videos. It never uploads, copies or shares them. If a photo is stored in iCloud Photos, iOS may download it from your own iCloud account so it can be displayed; Sift itself does not contact any server.

What stays on your device

Your keep and bin decisions and your settings are stored only on your iPhone.

Deleting

Photos are deleted only when you confirm in the iOS prompt. They go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

No tracking

Sift has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Children

Sift collects no data from anyone, including children.
